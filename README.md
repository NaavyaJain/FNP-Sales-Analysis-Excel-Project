## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from Ferns N Petals (FNP) using Microsoft Excel. The analysis focuses on customer purchasing behavior, revenue trends, product performance, delivery efficiency, and occasion-based sales patterns.

An interactive dashboard was created using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Slicers, Timelines, and KPI Cards to answer key business questions and provide actionable insights.

## The dataset consists of three tables:

### Customers
- Customer ID
- Customer Name

- Order ID
- Customer ID

- Delivery Date

## Business Questions Answered
### Q1. Which month generated the highest and lowest revenue?
- The month of August generated the highest revenue of ₹ 737,389, contributing approximately 20.94% of the total revenue.
- The month of January generated the highest revenue of ₹ 95,468, contributing approximately  2.71% of the total revenue.
### Q2. Which occasion has the highest revenue contribution?		
- Anniversary generated the highest revenue of ₹ 674,634.00, contributing approximately 19.16% of the total revenue.
### Q3. Which product generated the highest revenue?
- Magnam Set generated the highest revenue of ₹ 121,905, contributing approximately 3.46% of total revenue. 
### Q4. During which hour are the most orders placed?
- Highest number of orders are placed around 19:00 i.e, 7:00 PM				
### 5. City with Highest Orders & Revenue
- Among the selected Top 10 cities, Imphal has the highest number of orders, with 29 orders, and also generates the highest revenue of ₹125,854. However, the rankings of other cities show that cities with more orders do not always generate more revenue. For example, Kavali has fewer orders than Dhanbad but generates higher revenue, indicating a higher average order value. Therefore, city revenue is influenced by both order volume and the value of individual orders.
### Q6. Which occasion has the highest Average Order Value?		
- Raksha Bandhan has the highest Average Order Value of ₹ 4,784.73
### Q7. Which products have high sales volume but low revenue?			
- Cake has a relatively high sales volume of 375 units but generates only ₹329,862 in revenue, indicating comparatively low revenue per unit. Sweets also sold more units than Soft Toys, 541 versus 503, but generated slightly lower revenue, ₹733,842 versus ₹740,831. Therefore, Cake and Sweets can be considered categories with comparatively high sales volume but lower revenue performance.
### Q8. Does delivery time vary by city?	
- Among the selected Top 10 cities, average delivery time ranges from 6.50 to 8.25 days. Serampore has the longest average delivery time at 8.25 days, while Bharatpur, Khandwa, and Khora have the shortest at 6.50 days. The selected cities have an average delivery time of 6.94 days, compared with the overall dataset average of 5.53 days.
### Q9. Does delivery time vary by occasion?	
- Average delivery time varies slightly by occasion. Holi has the longest average delivery time at approximately 5.78 days, while Diwali has the shortest at approximately 4.95 days. The difference is only about 0.83 days, indicating that occasion has a relatively limited impact on delivery time.
### Q10. What % of total revenue comes from the Top 5 products? OR Is revenue concentrated among a small number of products?			
- The Top 5 products contribute approximately 15.40% to the total revenue.
### Q11. Which month has the highest number of orders vs highest revenue?			
- The month of August generated the highest revenue of ₹ 737,389
- The month of February has the highest number of orders, with 213 orders.
  
## Key Insights
- August generated the highest revenue despite February having the highest number of orders.
- Anniversary occasions contributed the largest share of revenue.
- Raksha Bandhan had the highest average order value.
- Most orders were placed around 7 PM.
- Revenue is distributed across products, with the Top 5 products contributing only 15.4%.
- Magnam Set generated ₹121,905, contributing 3.46% of total revenue.					
- Imphal had the highest order count and revenue.					
- Delivery time varied more by city than by occasion.
